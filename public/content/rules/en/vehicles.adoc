= Vehicles

Vehicles cover tanks, assault guns, tank destroyers, armoured cars, and self-propelled artillery.

== Armour

Vehicles use four armour facings:

* *Front*
* *Side*
* *Rear*
* *Exposed*

These values average the relevant protected areas of the vehicle when the hull, superstructure, turret, mantlet, or other parts differ.

Exposed armour represents the weakest vulnerable areas, such as top armour, hatches, ventilation systems, optics, open fighting compartments, or attacks resolved from above.

=== Calculating Armour Values

To keep the game values coherent with real data, effective armour can be estimated as follows:

*Effective armour = Scale x (armour in mm / cos(theta))*

For example, an 80 mm plate sloped at 55 degrees gives this unscaled value:

*Ef = 80 / cos(55 degrees) approximately 139 mm*
