= Infantry

Infantry squads are the backbone of most forces in Ashes of War. They are flexible, relatively cheap, and reliable for taking or holding objectives.

== Battlefield Role

Infantry operates best in cover, buildings, prepared positions, and close terrain. Infantry can assault, dig in, screen vehicles, and hold ground more efficiently than armoured units.

== Basic Profile

* *Movement:* 6"
* *Strengths:* Excellent in cover; can assault and dig in.
* *Weaknesses:* Vulnerable to indirect fire, suppression, and tank assaults.

== Bases

Infantry units are organised into bases. Each base defines the number of soldiers represented and the weapons assigned to that group.

== Common Actions

* Move
* Shoot
* Assault
* Dig in
* Rally or reorganise when affected by morale rules
