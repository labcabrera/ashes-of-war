= Introduction

Welcome to *Ashes of War*, a tabletop miniature wargame set in the European Theatre of World War II.

== Overview

Ashes of War is designed for 2 players. Each player commands an army composed of infantry squads, armoured vehicles, artillery batteries, and support elements. The goal is to achieve your mission objectives while neutralising the enemy force.

== Core Concepts

=== Command Points
Each turn you receive *Command Points (CP)* based on your army type and remaining resources. Spend CP to activate units, call in fire support, or trigger special abilities.

=== Activation
On your turn, select one of your units and activate it. An activated unit may:
* *Move* up to its Move value in inches.
* *Shoot* at an enemy unit in line of sight.
* *Assault* into close combat.
* *Dig In* to gain a defensive bonus.

=== Dice
Ashes of War uses standard six-sided dice (d6). Most actions require rolling against a target number; results equal to or higher than the target number succeed.

== Turn Structure

. *Initiative* — Both players roll 1d6. The higher result goes first (re-roll ties).
. *Activation Phase* — Players alternate activating units until all units have been activated.
. *Rally Phase* — Suppressed units attempt to recover.
. *Resource Phase* — Collect income and replenish command points.
. *End Phase* — Check victory conditions.

== Victory Conditions

Scenarios define specific objectives. Common objectives include:
* *Hold the Line* — Control more objectives than the enemy at game end.
* *Annihilate* — Destroy 75 % or more of the enemy's points value.
* *Recon* — Move a unit off the enemy's table edge.
